# Bandit audit, 2026-10-24

Second full audit, covering everything since `AUDIT-2026-10-11.md` (web `ecb1ebd..bbdb64a`,
28 commits, 62 files; the Swift port since its first push, which had never been audited).
Nothing was edited except this file, the two state documents and one new review script.
Four reviewers covered the Supabase backend, the React UI, the game and money logic in
`src/lib` (with a 7,000-rounds-per-game randomised run), and the iPhone, lock-screen and
watch code in `../bandit-swift`; the live project was inspected read-only.

Everything mechanical is green: `tsc -b`, `oxlint` (15 warnings, none a bug), `npm test`
(all checks), `vite build` (one 1.12 MB chunk, up from 952 KB), `swift test` (66 tests).
The three Edge Functions deployed in prod match the repo line for line. The eight apply
files since the last audit are all in prod and all in `schema.sql`. No errors in the last
24 hours of Supabase logs. Advisors: only the expected notes.

The short version: the sync work of the last two weeks is sound where it was aimed (scores
merge hole by hole, own holes win, bookings stay out of stats, every new column maps both
ways) and the money in all fourteen games nets to zero over 105,000 random rounds. The
holes are around the edges of that work: writes that still go out whole, two phones
crossing the same line, and an iPhone outbox that does not drain after a relaunch.

## Fix before the next push and TestFlight build

Ranked by what it costs on a real Sunday. "Both" means the web app and the iPhone app.

1. **The iPhone outbox never flushes after a relaunch.** Both the launch-time flush and the
   reconnect flush return early at `Bandit/Data/Outbox.swift:86`
   (`guard !queue.isEmpty, let latest`): `latest` is an in-memory snapshot that only
   `apply()` sets, so a queue restored from disk waits until the user makes some other
   edit. Scenario: three holes scored with no signal, iOS kills the app overnight (or
   relaunches it for the watch), opened next morning on wifi: the banner says "3 changes
   saved on this phone" and nothing goes out. Fix: drop the `let latest` guard and pass an
   empty `AppData` (the Supabase backend ignores `next`). Swift only. CONFIRMED.

2. **Every live-card save rewrites every bet row whole, including the game setup, from
   that phone's copy.** `src/pages/HoleEntry.tsx:309-312` calls `updateBet` for every bet
   on each 800 ms save and `supabaseBackend.ts:165-172` sends `game` with it; the iPhone
   does the same (`Bandit/Store.swift:349-355`, `BetWrite`). Scenario: Matt taps the
   wolf's call on the 5th; Barry's phone, loaded before that tap, types a score a second
   later and upserts the wolf bet with its stale picks: the call is gone. After any
   offline stretch the outbox replays every stale bet and wipes every call, press, pick,
   claim and hammer made meanwhile. Fix: a `bet.results` change kind that updates only
   `results` (skipped when unchanged), so the setup editors are the only writers of
   `game`. Both. CONFIRMED.

3. **Any non-score edit to a round still writes the whole `rounds` row from that phone's
   copy.** `roundPatch` sets `roundChanged` and the backend upserts `roundRow(r)` (every
   column: photos, green fee, ghosts, tee, notes, trip, nines). Callers: ghost pick, green
   fee, booking edits, photo add and remove. Scenario: Matt, offline on the 9th, picks a
   ghost (queued); Barry adds two photos and Pat sets the green fee; Matt's queue flushes
   and both are gone (the photo files stay in the bucket, orphaned). The 2026-10-22 merge
   fixed this for score saves only. Fix: diff the top-level fields in `roundPatch` and
   send only the changed columns with `update().eq('id')`. Both. CONFIRMED.

4. **Coming back to the foreground replaces local state while this phone's writes are
   still queued.** Web `store.tsx:222-238` (`visibilitychange` load, `setData(fresh)`),
   iPhone `Bandit/Store.swift:76-82` with `BanditApp.swift:107-114` firing the flush and
   the refresh concurrently. The live card drops its edits as soon as the optimistic store
   agrees, so the three queued holes show blank, the pad jumps back, the scorer re-enters
   them, and the "Matt: 5 on the 7th" note rings for the phone's own score when the queue
   lands. On the iPhone with item 1 the blanks are permanent. Fix: skip or defer the reload
   while writes are pending (`outboxStatus().pending > 0` / `outbox.pending > 0`), or lay
   the queued patches onto the loaded data with `applyScorePatch` first. Both. PLAUSIBLE,
   timing.

5. **A hammer thrown and never answered settles at the hammered value.** The UI stores
   `doubles + 1` with `pending` set when a side hammers (`HoleEntry.tsx:862`);
   `bets.ts:1134` reads `doubles` and ignores `pending` (`Bets.swift:1046` the same). $1
   hammer, hole 1 hammered and unanswered, a 3 against a 4: "Took the 1st at ×2", $2 paid
   where nobody accepted. Fix: value the hole at `doubles - (pending ? 1 : 0)`, or leave it
   unjudged until answered. Both. CONFIRMED by script.

6. **A press on the 1st or 10th is a second front or back nine, and one hole can be
   pressed twice.** The Press button shows on every hole (`HoleEntry.tsx:756-767`);
   `bets.ts:448-459` makes a leg of every stored press with no dedupe and no check that the
   press starts after the nine does. A wins 3-9, B wins 1-2, presses on 1, 5 and 5 again at
   $10: A is paid $50 where $30 is right. Fix: hide Press on each nine's first hole, and in
   `calcNassauMatch` (both apps) dedupe the presses and drop one on the nine's first hole.
   Both. CONFIRMED by script.

7. **A stake with three decimals creates money.** `BetEditor.tsx:108` saves
   `Number(stake)` and the input accepts "0.125"; each golfer's amount is then rounded on
   its own (`bets.ts:36`). Four golfers, skins at $0.125, one skin: the results sum to
   +2 cents. `BetEditorSheet.swift` parses its stake the same way. Fix: round the stake to
   cents on save and refuse a third decimal. Both. CONFIRMED by script.

8. **A paper-card scan overwrites holes a golfer entered on their own phone, and the
   wrong number sticks for the session.** `applyScan` (`HoleEntry.tsx:535-551`) writes an
   edit for every scanned cell with no `enteredByThemselves` check; the store's
   `guardOwnHoles` reverts it, so the store never agrees with the edit and the
   drop-when-landed logic never removes it. Matt's card shows Pat's 7th as 6 where Pat
   entered 5, every later save re-sends it, and Matt's live bets and the Final push use
   the 6. Fix: skip self-entered cells in `applyScan`, and in `landed` drop an edit for any
   hole flagged in that golfer's `selfHoles`. Web only (the iPhone scan path was not
   checked for this). CONFIRMED.

9. **Two gaps in `merge_round_scores`** (`supabase/apply-2026-10-24-own-scores.sql`, same
   in prod; needs a paste).
   - `puttsMode: 'off'` sets `p := '{}'` for anyone, with no `mine` or `self_holes` check,
     while individual putts and holes are protected (`:55-60`). Pat tracks his own putts,
     12 in; Matt's phone, still listing Pat, flips putts off: Pat's 12 entries are wiped.
     Client side `guardOwnHoles` protects per-hole putts but not the mode either. Fix: skip
     `puttsMode` when `not mine and true = any(f)`. CONFIRMED.
   - A client total is honoured over a card the server knows is incomplete (`:70-75`):
     `grossSet` is taken whenever the golfer has no self holes, and the recomputation only
     runs on a full merged card. Pat's 7th was cleared on phone A; phone B, stale with the
     7th still filled, enters Pat's 18th and sends `gross: 88`; the server stores 88 over
     a 17-hole card, `roundIsFinal` turns true everywhere, trophy pushes and the Saddam fire
     on an incomplete round. Fix: when the patch carries holes and the merged card is not
     complete, set the total null. CONFIRMED path, needs a stale phone.

10. **The iPhone treats any error mentioning "host", "connection", "network" or
    "internet" as being offline** (`Bandit/Data/Backend.swift:140`), and an offline error
    keeps the change at the head of the queue forever (`Outbox.swift:95`). A server refusal
    that quotes a column or value with one of those words in it (the `ghosts` column, a
    course named "Ghost Creek", a pool message) blocks everything behind it and the pending
    banner never clears. The web's list is deliberately narrower (`failed to fetch`,
    `networkerror`, `load failed`, `timeout`, `econn`, `offline`). Fix: match the web's
    list and rely on `URLError` and the 401 codes. Swift only. PLAUSIBLE.

## Worth fixing soon

Two phones, one line:

11. **The "final" moment is only seen by a phone whose own save crosses it.**
    `announceTrophies` (`store.tsx:192`) and the Final push (`HoleEntry.tsx:316-327`)
    compare `before` and `after` on this phone. Matt enters Pat's 18th while Barry enters
    his own within the reload window: neither save sees the round go final, so no trophy
    sheet, no trophy pushes and no "Final at" push, ever. The mirror case in
    `spectatorNews` sends "at the turn" twice. Fix: detect transitions against loaded data
    too, with a per-round "announced" marker, or move these pushes to a server trigger.
    Both. PLAUSIBLE.
12. **Spectator pushes are computed per phone**, so two scorers crossing the same lead
    change both push, and a phone missing holes 5 and 6 pushes the wrong totals; a hole
    cleared and re-entered re-fires it (`store.tsx:277`, `spectate.ts:70-71`). Both.
13. **Trophy moments re-fire** when a final round's hole is cleared and re-entered
    (`store.tsx:193` guards only on `before`). Both.
14. **Stored bet results come from the saving phone's copy of the card, not the merged
    one.** With two phones each keeping some golfers, the last save may settle from a
    partial card; standings money, Settle up and Venmo read `bets.results`
    (`stats.ts:126`, `settlements.ts:20`) while the round page recomputes live and
    disagrees. Fix: settle from the loaded round where money is read, or re-run and persist
    after the final reload. Both.

iPhone, lock screen and watch:

15. **GPS keeps running after the Live Activity ends.** `Location.swift:65-80` is only
    stopped by the card's `onDisappear` when the round is not live, so a round abandoned at
    the 12th, or one finished from the lock screen, keeps best-accuracy background location
    on for up to eight hours with the blue pill up. Fix: stop it when the activity ends or
    is dismissed (`LiveRounds.swift:220-243`). CONFIRMED by reading.
16. **The lock-screen inbox is cleared before the score is durable.** `drainInbox`
    (`LiveRounds.swift:189-191`) reads then writes an empty file on the main actor while
    `save` (`Shared/LiveRound.swift:159-187`) appends on the intent's executor; two fast
    Saves can lose one, and a background wake killed mid-request loses the score, since
    only a connectivity failure reaches the outbox file. Fix: one actor for the inbox,
    remove drained entries by value, and persist before sending for inbox-sourced writes.
17. **A queued watch score lands unconditionally when it finally arrives**
    (`WatchLink.swift:57-64`, `LiveRounds.swift:106-113`): a transfer queued out of reach
    overwrites a newer score the phone entered meanwhile and drags the card back to that
    hole; one arriving after the round is final changes a finished round. Fix: stamp each
    `WatchScore` with a time; ignore a stale one for a filled cell and never move the
    pointer from it.
18. **A roster change mid-round breaks the lock screen.** The activity's `golfers` are
    fixed at start (`LiveRounds.swift:59-83`) while `state()` indexes by the current
    players; after the booker adds or removes someone the names and columns mismatch, a
    Save files under the wrong id, and a deleted round's activity is never ended. Fix: end
    and restart the activity when the ids differ or the round is gone.
19. **The phone's scoring picker can deselect everyone** (`HoleEntryView.swift:640-650`);
    the watch's forbids it. With `asks` empty, `WatchRound.complete` is true on every hole,
    so the watch ends the golf workout and shows "Every card is in." mid-round, and the
    lock screen shows no entry row. The web has the matching oddity: "Scoring: nobody" while
    the pad still walks everyone (`HoleEntry.tsx:388`). Fix: refuse an empty pick (keep at
    least you), and base `complete` on cards, not asks. Both.
20. **The APNs token is not removed on sign-out or re-filed for the next account**
    (`Push.swift:46-62, 108-120`): sign out, sign in as someone else on the same phone,
    and the row keeps the old `player_id`. Fix: `removeDeviceToken` on sign-out and always
    upsert under the current player when the switch is on. Swift.
21. **One outbox and photo-outbox file per phone across accounts and modes**
    (`Outbox.swift:37-39`, `PhotoOutbox.swift:33-34`), and `stopListening()` has no
    caller, so the realtime channel outlives sign-out. A simulator run on the real group
    leaves queued writes that a `-local` relaunch "applies" to the demo snapshot and drops;
    writes queued as A go out as B. Fix: key the files by mode and player id; call
    `stopListening()` and cancel the path monitor from `signOut`. Swift.
22. **The persisted queue decodes all-or-nothing** (`Outbox.swift:43`): a build that adds
    a `Change` case or a non-optional field drops every pending write silently on update.
    Fix: decode entries individually. Swift.
23. **A new round's upsert and its first patch go out concurrently** (`Store.swift:229-234`;
    the web's outbox only serialises once something is queued): if the upsert times out
    and the patch gets through, the patch is refused on the foreign key and dropped, and
    those holes never resend. Fix: serialise direct sends per entity. Both.

Smaller:

24. `bet.game` can never be cleared on the server: `supabaseBackend.ts:181` omits the
    column when the bet has no setup, so turning a match-play Nassau back to stroke play
    would revert on reload. Latent (no editor does it yet). Send `game: null` now that the
    column is in prod.
25. `guardOwnHoles` (`scoringFor.ts:69-74`) keeps a stale 18-hole gross on a card it has
    just made incomplete; the server does the same. Keep null when the incoming gross was
    null and the guarded card is not full.
26. "+ Putts" overrides (`HoleEntry.tsx:83, 203-208`) are session-long and never
    reconciled with another phone's choice, so two phones can flip-flop a golfer's putts
    on and off. Clear the override once `rp.putts` reflects it.
27. Green fee amount is not rounded to cents (`GreenFees.tsx:59`): $33.333 a head leaves a
    cent on the payer nobody owes. `bets.ts:36` can yield `-0` (44 of 105,000 random
    rounds; nothing visible today). Round both.
28. A golfer removed then re-added is out of every card bet for good (results are rebuilt
    from `entries` and persisted). Keep the bet's ids and write 0 for the absentee.
29. Junk's birdie kind on a course without full pars counts nothing silently
    (`bets.ts:1059`, same in Swift). Add a note like Snake's.
30. Swift decodes an unknown bet type as `custom` (`SupabaseBackend.swift:233`); the web
    keeps the type. An edit on the iPhone would convert the row for good.
31. Golfers added on a booking are appended, then re-sorted to roster order on every load
    (`BookedRound.tsx:276-281`, `Store.swift:308-318`). Sort on save.
32. Hardening in `merge_round_scores`: hole keys outside 0..17 extend the arrays
    (`:45-49`), and the `round_players` policy accepts a `player_id` from another group
    (prod has none). Range-check the key; add `player_id in (select id from players)`.
33. `notify` has no per-caller throttle; a chirp is one push each. Four friends, so this is
    tolerance, not security.

## Verified fine

- Schema: every column the eight apply files add is in prod and in `schema.sql`, mapped
  both ways in both apps (`tee_time`, `booked_by`, `green_fee`, `cover`, `stories`,
  `muted_rounds`, `chirps_off`, `self_holes`, `game`); `chirps` and `apns_tokens` are under
  RLS with group-scoped or own-row policies; `chirps` is in the realtime publication; all
  three storage policy sets are `to authenticated` and folder-scoped; the apply files are
  idempotent. `merge_round_scores` in prod matches the apply file; it is `security
  invoker`, anon is revoked, a non-member hits RLS, golfer removal is explicit and computed
  from this phone's own `before`, the `rounds` row is untouched by a pure score save, and
  `for update` locks are taken in roster order so concurrent saves cannot deadlock (keep
  that invariant: a deadlock error would be dropped as poison).
- Edge Functions: `notify` v9 still requires `claim_my_player`, intersects recipients with
  the caller's group, drops the caller, builds the APNs JWT correctly (ES256, cached 40
  min), prunes 410 / BadDeviceToken / Unregistered, degrades to web push without the key
  or table, forces `url` to start with `/`. `scan-card` and `course-lookup` keep the
  player-row gate, the daily cap and input validation. `link-preview` keeps its origin,
  private-host and size checks (DNS rebinding still open from last time).
- Store and sync (web): `sortBookings` is idempotent, a round is never in both lists,
  bookings move on the first score and back if every hole is cleared, `deleteRound` clears
  both lists plus bets and payments; `roundPatch` diffs against the freshest state, an
  empty patch writes nothing, the first score on a booking sends only the merge call; the
  own-holes guard never blocks the golfer's own phone; re-entering a score another phone
  changed is sent; pushes fire only on transitions, never on load; foreground and
  subscribe listeners are cleaned up; hook order is right in every new page and the new
  inline editors call `useHoldUpdates()`; every new date comparison is local-calendar.
- Store and sync (iPhone): `ScorePatch`, `guardOwnHoles`, `asks`, `selfScoring`,
  `sortBookings` and the snake_case mapping match the web; `self_holes` is never written by
  the client; dates stay yyyy-mm-dd strings; money is integer cents through `jsRound`;
  `padded18` keeps 9- and 27-hole data in range; the force-unwraps in `Bets.swift` sit
  behind the right guards; `-local` sticks only in debug and `resetToSample` is local-only;
  the Live Activity writes the inbox before updating, keeps a tapped stepper value, and
  ends 30 minutes after the last card; `WatchHoleMap` round-trips in metres; the golf
  workout cannot double-end.
- Money: all fourteen games (skins, winner-take-all skins, both Nassaus with auto and hand
